# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who have had enough of stress, pain, anger, lack, or discontentment. They arrive exhausted by outer fixes and are open to a quieter invitation inward. Secondary users include the Eyes Closed client reviewing design directions and audiobook production (`/audio`, `/audio/editorial`, Design Lab).

## Product Purpose

Eyes Closed is a contemplative digital sanctuary for the Formless teaching. The site helps visitors feel seen, create a pause, land the central insight (you are the awareness behind the voice), and meet the book as a doorway; not a sales funnel. Success looks like spaciousness, recognition, and a natural invitation to stay close; never urgency or conversion pressure.

Internal audio review surfaces support the same brand tone while helping the author and production team compare masters, approve chapters, and understand mastering guidance before ACX upload.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, spacious. Studio, not storefront. Sanctuary, not billboard. Compassionate, clear, reflective, nonjudgmental, grounded.

## Anti-references

- Conventional sales pages, method brands, and conversion funnels
- Dense SaaS dashboards with identical card grids and hero-metric templates
- Purple AI gradients, glassmorphism stacks, neon accents
- Urgency chrome: pop-ups, countdown timers, sticky email banners
- Hype language (elevate, unleash, revolutionary, next-gen)
- DAW-heavy studio chrome on editorial listen surfaces

## Design Principles

- Practice what you preach: the interface should feel unrushed and honest
- Recognition before proof before action on public journeys
- Brand leads; tools serve; never let chrome overpower Formless / Eyes Closed
- Show the meaning of data in plain language before the metric name
- Polish, don't over-process: preserve natural narration when metrics already pass

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep body line length readable (≈65–75ch for prose). Provide text alternatives for chart meaning, not only color. Tap targets ≥44px on interactive controls. Semantic landmarks and labeled controls on review tools.
