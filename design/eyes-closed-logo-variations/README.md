# Eyes Closed Logo Variations

These variations evolve the crescent-and-particle mark from the supplied reference while adding the `Eyes Closed` name to the lockup.

## Refined Ember Direction

This round returns to the initial `04 Ember Frequency` direction. The crescent is now cream-only, while the particle system keeps the right-tip trail and borrows the canvas reference logic: a tighter arc-hugging flow, a comet tail from the back/right tip, mostly cream particles, and restrained sage/ember accents.

- `04-ember-frequency-lockup.svg`: primary refined direction.
- `04a-refined-frequency-flow.svg`: slightly tighter particle stream.
- `04b-refined-arc-hugging-particles.svg`: adds a subtle band of particles hugging the arc before the tail exits.
- `04c-typography-cormorant-light.svg`: lighter Cormorant-style serif.
- `04d-typography-garamond-book.svg`: smaller, quieter Garamond-style serif.
- `04e-typography-didot-editorial.svg`: high-contrast editorial serif.
- `04f-typography-humanist-sans.svg`: restrained all-caps humanist sans.

The `04` typography is now optically centered at the crescent center rather than the combined crescent-plus-particle-tail center.

## Codex Cut

This round intentionally stops treating the brief as a set of micro-corrections and pushes the identity system harder. Some directions are practical, some are too expressive for a final production mark, but each is meant to reveal a different emotional territory for `Eyes Closed`.

- `03a-author-cut-orbit-surge.svg`: the strongest cinematic identity direction; the mark feels like a still point inside a moving field.
- `03b-author-cut-eclipse-veil.svg`: darker, more sacred, and more iconic; good if the brand should feel quiet but powerful.
- `03c-author-cut-threshold-horizontal.svg`: the most usable header/navigation lockup from this round.
- `03d-author-cut-ritual-seal.svg`: app icon, seal, stamp, favicon, or book spine system.
- `03e-author-cut-wordmark-field.svg`: wordmark-led direction where the symbol becomes the pause between words.
- `03f-author-cut-void-bloom.svg`: most atmospheric and expressive; useful for hero graphics and brand motion.
- `03g-author-cut-quiet-lux.svg`: minimal premium direction, closer to a final luxury mark.

## Dynamic Particle Round

The next round restores the lower tagline and explores deeper particle systems inspired by the supplied atmosphere references. These keep the closed-eye/crescent concept intact, but add dimensional wave paths, dotted currents, foreground/background particles, sage depth, and subtle ember accents.

- `02a-dynamic-current-serif.svg`: strongest direct evolution of Source Particle.
- `02b-orbital-field-serif.svg`: more centered and dimensional, with a larger orbit field.
- `02c-breath-wave-sans.svg`: sans-serif option with a breath/wave motion system.
- `02d-particle-bloom-serif.svg`: denser, more emotional bloom from the right side of the crescent.
- `02e-quiet-depth-horizontal.svg`: horizontal/header lockup with dynamic particles and tagline.

## Design Review

The source mark is strong because it carries several useful readings at once: a closed eyelid, crescent moon, horizon, and release of subtle energy. The right-side particle field gives it motion and a high-frequency quality, but it needs intentional spacing, a lighter crescent, and a quiet wordmark relationship so it feels refined rather than decorative.

The most grounded directions are:

- `01-source-particle-horizontal.svg`: closest to the supplied screenshot, with a refined particle arc and centered wordmark.
- `02-centered-orbit-lockup.svg`: strongest brand-system bridge, tying the mark back to the construction/orbit language from the existing Eyes Closed board.
- `03-grounded-horizon-lockup.svg`: calmest and most stable option; good for premium headers and print.
- `04-ember-frequency-lockup.svg`: warm/high-frequency option using the existing ember accent.
- `05-monogram-seal-lockup.svg`: compact seal direction for favicons, app icons, or small stamps.
- `06-minimal-wordmark-lockup.svg`: most restrained wordmark-led lockup.
