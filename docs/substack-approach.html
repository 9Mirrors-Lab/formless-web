Open questions

Decisions to make with Soni before committing.

Research checklist
  • Eyes Closed vs Soni C. vs Formless as the Substack publication name
  • Free only vs paid tier later; what belongs behind paywall if anything
  • Cadence: weekly, biweekly, or seasonal bursts tied to book milestones
  • What can be excerpted from the manuscript vs net-new writing
  • How Substack links to the website (footer, about, book CTA) without feeling like a funnel
  • Overlap with video framework posts (YouTube + Substack or Substack-first)
  • Launch timing relative to IngramSpark and site rebrand
