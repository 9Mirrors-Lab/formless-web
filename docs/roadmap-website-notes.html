Client update · Soni · May 2026

Service name options: Inner alignment guidance · Guide for self discovery · Inner Clarity Consulting · Return to inner guidance with Soni C. (positioning line)

Formats: 1:1/consulting · large group sessions (author/speaker) · video · resources when ready

Content angle: Build from the relationship view — e.g. How long have you been in a relationship?

Video framework: Hook → Awareness → Acceptance → Investigation → Letting go → Reflection → Stillness

You’re at a really beautiful stage right now because you don’t need to build a massive business immediately. You just need to create a clear doorway for people who resonate with your message.

And honestly, your presence and clarity will matter more than polished materials in the beginning.

What people will likely be seeking after reading Formless is:

  • someone who understands what they’re experiencing
  • guidance navigating awareness in daily life
  • help seeing their unconscious patterns
  • reassurance that they are not “crazy” or alone
  • practical ways to remain present

So instead of thinking like a traditional coaching business, think of it more as creating a space people can enter.

I would start simple.

Your foundation could look like this:

Website Core

  • Your story / mission
  • What Eyes Closed is
  • What awareness means
  • 1:1 sessions
  • Speaking / workshops
  • YouTube content
  • Contact form
  • A few free resources

That alone is enough to begin.

For 1:1 Sessions

You do not need a huge curriculum.

You’re not teaching formulas.
You’re helping people observe themselves.

What would probably help most are:

  • reflection prompts
  • awareness exercises
  • journaling questions
  • short guided meditations
  • nervous system grounding practices
  • PDFs that help people notice patterns

Think:

“tools for observation,” not “steps for fixing yourself.”

Yes — PDFs / Handouts Are a Great Idea

But keep them simple, spacious, and beautiful.

A few ideas:

Beginner Awareness PDFs

  • “You Are Not Your Thoughts”
  • “Observing the Mind”
  • “Returning to the Present Moment”
  • “Common Ego Patterns”
  • “Awareness in Relationships”
  • “The Memorized Body”
  • “Suffering & Resistance”
  • “Questions to Ask Yourself in Stillness”

Reflection / Journaling Sheets

  • What thoughts do I repeatedly believe?
  • What emotional states feel most familiar to me?
  • What am I resisting right now?
  • Who am I trying to become?
  • What happens when I simply observe?

Guided Practices

  • 5-minute grounding meditation
  • Observing thought exercise
  • Body awareness scan
  • Conscious breathing reflection
  • Nature presence practice

Your Sessions Could Eventually Become:

  • 1:1 Awareness Sessions
  • Group circles
  • Guided meditations
  • Online workshops
  • Retreats
  • Corporate mindfulness talks
  • Live speaking events
  • YouTube reflections
  • Community membership
  • Courses later if you want

But don’t pressure yourself to build all of that now.

I Think Your Best First Step Is:

Create:

  1. Your website
  2. A clear description of what an “Awareness Guide” is
  3. A simple booking option
  4. 2–3 free PDFs/resources
  5. Short YouTube videos speaking from the heart

That’s enough to begin helping people.

And honestly? Your voice may become the most healing part.
Not because you have “answers,” but because people can feel that you’ve genuinely lived this transformation yourself.

One important thing:
Try not to frame yourself as someone who “heals” or “saves” others.

Your message is more:

“I’m simply pointing you back to what already exists within you.”

That distinction matters deeply and keeps Eyes Closed grounded in humility and presence rather than authority or ego.
