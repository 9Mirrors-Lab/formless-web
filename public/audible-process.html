Kindle ebook first

ACX explicitly requires the ebook to already sit in the Amazon.com bookstore. Searching ACX before that listing exists will look like the title is missing. Official: Claim my title, Kindle preorder, release options.

Preferred · September 1 preorder

Set the Kindle ebook for a September 1 preorder. After Amazon approves it, the book gets a public Amazon detail page and a Kindle ASIN before release. Customers can preorder immediately. That public listing is what ACX needs.

  • Final manuscript must be submitted more than 72 hours before September 1.
  • You cannot change the manuscript or book details inside that last 72 hours.
  • Miss the deadline and Amazon cancels the preorder and blocks new preorders for one year.

Alternative · publish now

Publish the Kindle ebook immediately. It becomes available for purchase before the official September 1 launch. Amazon generally takes up to 72 hours to review and publish it.

  • Use this only if you accept the ebook being buyable before launch day.
  • The ASIN and public page still appear after review, same as preorder.

Wait, then copy the ASIN

  1. Open the KDP Bookshelf.
  2. Locate Formless. Status should be Live, or a live preorder.
  3. Copy the ebook ASIN. It usually begins with B0.
  4. Confirm View on Amazon opens a public Amazon.com page.
  5. Only then go to ACX and claim with that ASIN.

Cover art

ACX quality-checks the square independently of the audio, often up to 10 business days. Title and author on the image must match the claim exactly. Official spec: ACX cover art requirements.

Formless
Sonika Cottman
Keep this corner clear if exclusive
  • True square, at least 2400 × 2400 px. No letterbox bars to fake a square.
  • JPG, PNG, or TIF. RGB, 24-bit, ≥ 72 dpi. File under 8 MB.
  • Title and author readable at thumbnail size. No blur, no pixelated type.
  • Filename: condensed title, alphanumeric only. Example Formless.jpg.
  • No Audible logos, watermarks, barcodes, QR codes, prices, runtimes, or other storefronts.
  • No CD jewel case, cellophane, print jacket (front+back), or photos of physical media.
  • Exclusive deals: Audible may add an “Only from Audible” triangle 41% in from the bottom-right. Leave that corner quiet.

On the ACX title

  • Book title, author, narrator, imprint (Eyes Closed).
  • Copyright year and copyright holder.
  • Language: English. Categories / BISAC that match the print book.
  • Short description (store blurb). Same story as the print listing.
  • Distribution: exclusive (higher royalty, Audible corner badge) or non-exclusive.
  • Territory rights you actually own.
  • Retail sample: 1–5 minutes. Set it from Introduction if Opening Credits is too short.
  • No music bed unless the manuscript calls for it.

Human review still fails clean numbers

Automated QC only checks the table above. Reviewers fail files for room jumps, NR shimmer, mouth noise, chair sounds, pumping, and hard cuts. Spot-check 2–3 minutes from the middle of each chapter before upload.

Listen for

  • Same room character chapter to chapter.
  • Head and tail are room tone, not digital mute.
  • No lip smacks, paper, HVAC bursts, or keyboard.
  • Breaths present but not gasping.

Processing tells

  • Metallic or watery noise-reduction haze.
  • Compressor pumping on inhales.
  • Thin “radio” voice from an aggressive high-pass.
  • Echo or room ring that a studio take would not have.
