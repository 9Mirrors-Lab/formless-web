Kindle ebook first

    

      ACX will not find Formless until a public Amazon.com ebook listing exists. Searching ACX before that looks like the title is missing. Official: Claim my title, Kindle preorder, release options.
    

    

      

        
Preferred · September 1 preorder

        
Set the Kindle ebook for a September 1 preorder. After Amazon approves it, you get a public Amazon detail page and a Kindle ASIN before release. That public listing is what ACX needs.

        
    
          
  • Final manuscript must be submitted more than 72 hours before September 1.
    • 
          
  • You cannot change the manuscript or book details inside that last 72 hours.
    • 
          
  • Miss the deadline and Amazon cancels the preorder and blocks new preorders for one year.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Alternative · publish now

        
Publish the Kindle ebook immediately. It becomes buyable before the official September 1 launch. Amazon generally takes up to 72 hours to review and publish it.

        
    
          
  • Use this only if you accept the ebook being buyable before launch day.
    • 
          
  • The ASIN and public page still appear after review, same as preorder.
    • 
        

      

    

    

      
Wait, then copy the ASIN

      
    
        
  1. Open the KDP Bookshelf.
    2. 
        
  2. Locate Formless. Status should be Live, or a live preorder.
    3.

    4.

    5.

Audio tracks

    

      Submit the ACX masters from Drive, not the listen-page stream. Every file uses the same technical window. Opening and closing files also carry spoken credits.
    

    

      Google Drive · ACX-Masters
    


    

      

        
Format

        
MP3 192 kbps CBR

        
WAV also accepted. We ship MP3.

        
Why it matters: VBR or a lower bitrate fails Audio Analysis in minutes.

      

      

        
Sample / channels

        
44100 Hz · mono

        
Stereo is rejected.

        
Why it matters: Stereo uploads are a hard portal reject, even when loudness is perfect.

      

      

        
RMS

        
−23 to −18 dBFS

        
Studio target −21 to −19.

        
Why it matters: Too quiet or too hot fails the ACX loudness window on every chapter.

      

      

        
True peak

        
≤ −3.0 dBFS

        
We encode at −3.5 to survive MP3.

        
Why it matters: Peaks above −3.0 clip after MP3 encode and trip the auto check.

      

      

        
Noise floor

        
≤ −60 dBFS

        
Measure the 0.75 s head, not narration.

        
Why it matters: A noisy head fails even when the spoken parts sound clean.

      

      

        
Room tone

        
0.5–1 s head · 1–5 s tail

        
We deliver 0.75 s / 2 s. No dead-zero silence. No gap > 5 s mid-file.

        
Why it matters: Dead-zero silence and long mid-file gaps are common reject reasons.

Cover art

    

      Submit file is ready: Formless.png sits in the ACX-Masters Drive folder with the chapter masters. ACX quality-checks the square independently of the audio, often up to 10 business days. Title and author on the image must match the claim exactly. Official spec: ACX cover art requirements.
    

    

      
      
    
        
  • In Drive Upload this file: Formless.png
    • 
        
  • Drive: ACX-Masters · Formless.png
    • 
        
  • 2400 × 2400, true square, PNG, 24-bit RGB, 96 dpi, 4.2 MB (cap is 8 MB).
    • 
        
  • Title FORMLESS and author SONIKA COTTMAN on the image. ACX title record must be Formless / Sonika Cottman.
    • 
        
  • No logos, barcodes, print jackets, letterbox bars, or physical-media mockups.
    • 
        
  • Exclusive deals: Audible may add an “Only from Audible” triangle 41% in from the bottom-right. The author line sits on that bottom band.
    •
