On the page

A clear first impression

  • Tab title — “Remembering Who You Are Beyond The Mind” sets the tone before a word is read.
  • Favicon — the Eyes Closed mark feels intentional in a sea of open tabs.
  • Calm structure — headings and sections that scan easily on phone and desktop.
  • Your voice — copy that sounds like Sonika, not a template.
Small details signal care. Care builds trust before someone reads a single paragraph.

Under the hood

Guidance for crawlers

  • robots.txt — tells search tools which pages matter (Home, Practice, Book, Science, About) and which to skip (sign-in, internal design pages).
  • Sitemap — coming soon: a simple list of every public URL worth indexing.
  • Private previews — pages like this one stay out of public search on purpose.

Rolling out

A welcome guide for AI

  • llms.txt — a short index at eyesclosed.love/llms.txt: who you are, which pages matter, and how to describe the work accurately.
  • llms-full.txt — the full public teaching copy, organized by page, so AI answers stay aligned with the live site.
  • Updated on deploy — when your words change, the guides refresh automatically.
Think of it as a calm front door for assistants, not a trick to game rankings.

Listening with care

Learning what resonates

  • Privacy-conscious analytics show which pages people visit and how they move through the site.
  • No change to how visitors experience Eyes Closed — this is a quiet layer for the team.
  • Insights inform better structure and copy over time, always in service of clarity.

Reality check

SEO rewards pages that answer the question

Nobody knows every detail of Google's algorithm. What we do know: helpful, trustworthy pages that people actually read tend to rise over time.

For Eyes Closed, that means depth, honesty, and a site that feels as calm as the teaching.

What we are not doing

No tricks. No spam.

  • No flooding the site with hundreds of thin pages overnight.
  • No buying cheap backlinks or “guaranteed ranking” packages.
  • No keyword stuffing or copy that sounds like a machine wrote it.
  • No pressure tactics — discovery should feel like an invitation, not a funnel.
