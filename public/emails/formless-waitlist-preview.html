About the book

Formless is an invitation to explore the stillness beneath a busy mind. Sonika Cottman guides a journey of self-discovery: peace and clarity by observing thought without identification.

The book looks at consciousness, the illusion of the self, and the liberating truth of our essential being.

From Soni

This work is born from years of personal practice and guiding others toward inner freedom. It is a love letter to the quiet intelligence that resides within each of us, waiting to be recognized.

I poured my heart into these pages, hoping to offer a steady hand to anyone seeking who they truly are beyond the constant chatter of the mind. May it be a source of peace for you.
