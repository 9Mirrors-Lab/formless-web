About the book

Formless is a journey into the stillness under the busy mind. It looks at consciousness, the illusion of the self, and the quieter truth of who you already are.

The work comes from years of practice and of walking with others toward inner freedom. It is a love letter to the quiet intelligence that lives in each of us, waiting to be recognized.

A note from Soni

I poured my heart into these pages, hoping to offer a steady hand to anyone seeking who they truly are beyond the chatter of the mind. May it be a source of peace for you.
